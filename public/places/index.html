Holy places of Makkah and Madinah

History, what to know before you visit, and directions on Google Maps for 13 places in Makkah and Madinah.

General information. History is summarised from established sources, and visiting rules come from official guidance, which can change. For religious guidance, consult a qualified scholar.

Makkah

  • Masjid al-Haram المسجد الحرام
  • Mina منى
  • Arafat and Jabal al-Rahmah عرفات وجبل الرحمة
  • Muzdalifah مزدلفة
  • Jabal al-Nour and Cave Hira جبل النور وغار حراء
  • Jabal Thawr جبل ثور
  • Jannat al-Mu'alla مقبرة المعلاة

Madinah

  • Masjid an-Nabawi (the Prophet's Mosque) المسجد النبوي
  • Masjid Quba مسجد قباء
  • Masjid al-Qiblatayn مسجد القبلتين
  • Mount Uhud and the Martyrs of Uhud جبل أحد وشهداء أحد
  • Jannat al-Baqi بقيع الغرقد
  • The Seven Mosques (Khandaq) المساجد السبعة
