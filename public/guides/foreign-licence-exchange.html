Driving

Exchanging a foreign licence

Holders from about 48 approved countries can exchange without a road test.

General information, not advice. This page summarises publicly available rules in plain language. It is not legal advice or a recommendation to take any action. Procedures, fees and fines change without notice and can differ by case. The official portal below is the only authoritative source.

You need: Iqama, passport, original foreign licence (with Arabic translation if required), medical report.

Steps

  1. Check whether your country is on the exchange list at the traffic department (Muroor) or in Absher.
  2. Do the medical and eye test at an approved centre.
  3. Book a Muroor appointment in Absher and pay the fee through SADAD.
  4. Take your documents to the Muroor office. Most exchanges do not need a driving test.
  5. If your country is not on the list, follow the driving school route instead.

Last checked: 2026-10-24. Confirm current rules on the official source: absher.sa
