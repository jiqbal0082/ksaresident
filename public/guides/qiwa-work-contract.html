Check and accept your work contract on Qiwa

See the contract your employer registered before you accept it.

  • Last checked: 5 Nov 2026
  • Work & rights

General information, not advice. This page summarises publicly available rules in plain language. It is not legal advice or a recommendation to take any action. Procedures, fees and fines change without notice and can differ by case. The official portal below is the only authoritative source.

You need: Your Qiwa Individuals login.

Key figures

Who starts it
The employer creates the contract on Qiwa and sends it to the employee. Alerts come by SMS and on Qiwa.
What the employee can do
Approve it, reject it, or request an amendment on Qiwa Individuals. If both sides agree, HRSD treats it as certified.
Non-Saudi workers (Article 37)
The contract must be written and fixed-term. If no term is stated, it is one year from the actual start date and renews for a similar period if work continues.
Copies (Article 51)
Written in two copies, and each side keeps one. HRSD's guide says it is documented through the ministry's approved platform.

Steps

  1. Go to qiwa.sa and log in to your Individuals account.
  2. Open Services, then Employment Contracts.
  3. Read the details your employer entered: job title, contract length, working hours, pay and any added items.
  4. Choose to approve, reject or request an amendment.
  5. Keep a copy of the contract.
