Strictly prohibited

Goods and acts that are banned or tightly controlled, from customs, medicine and labour rules.

  • Last checked: 12 Nov 2026
  • Laws & conduct

General information, not advice. This page summarises publicly available rules in plain language. It is not legal advice or a recommendation to take any action. Procedures, fees and fines change without notice and can differ by case. The official portal below is the only authoritative source.

Steps

  1. Customs (ZATCA) lists intoxicants, such as alcohol, and drugs of all kinds as prohibited goods for travellers, and says fines apply to anyone who tries to bring them in.
  2. Never carry a bag, parcel or medicine for someone else, or a bag you did not pack yourself.
  3. Pills, creams, tonics, herbs and lotions that are not registered with the Saudi Food and Drug Authority (SFDA) are also on the ZATCA prohibited list.
  4. Medicines that contain narcotic or psychotropic substances need an SFDA clearance permit when you enter or leave Saudi Arabia. Apply on the Controlled Drugs System (cds.sfda.gov.sa) with the prescription and a medical report.
  5. Labor Law Article 39 (HRSD): a worker may not work for another employer or for their own account except under the prescribed rules and procedures.

Sources

Last checked: 12 Nov 2026. Confirm current rules on the official source:

  • zatca.gov.sa (prohibited goods)
  • ZATCA, prohibited goods for travellers
  • SFDA, clearance permit for controlled drugs (14 Aug 2025)
  • HRSD, employment of non-Saudis (Labor Law Art. 32 to 41)
