Work & rights

Resign the right way (2025 rules)

Since 19 February 2025 the Labor Law defines resignation and sets a 30-day response window.

General information, not advice. This page summarises publicly available rules in plain language. It is not legal advice or a recommendation to take any action. Procedures, fees and fines change without notice and can differ by case. The official portal below is the only authoritative source.

You need: Your contract details, notice period and planned last working day.

Steps

  1. Submit your resignation in writing. Where your employer uses Qiwa, submit it there so it is recorded.
  2. Your employer can accept it, delay it, or reject it.
  3. If the employer does not respond within 30 days, the resignation is treated as accepted.
  4. Serve your notice period as your contract and the law require.
  5. Use the End of service calculator to check your award before your final settlement.

Last checked: 2026-10-24. Confirm current rules on the official source: hrsd.gov.sa
