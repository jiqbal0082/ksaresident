Airports, terminals and parking

Find the right terminal, get there, and avoid drop-off and parking fees.

  • Last checked: 14 Nov 2026
  • Travel & flights

General information, not advice. This page summarises publicly available rules in plain language. It is not legal advice or a recommendation to take any action. Procedures, fees and fines change without notice and can differ by case. The official portal below is the only authoritative source.

You need: Your boarding pass or airline app (it shows your terminal).

Key figures

Airport status (reported, 12 to 14 Nov 2026)
Changes daily. Confirm your flight with your airline before you leave for the airport.
Riyadh (RUH)
Partly operating. Some Saudi airline flights for Riyadh still go through Qassim (ELQ) airport, with buses from Riyadh (reported 13 Nov). Some foreign airlines have restarted Riyadh flights, for example Qatar Airways from 13 Nov (reported 13 to 14 Nov).
Jeddah (JED)
Operating. Several foreign airlines fly there again; some others have paused Jeddah flights into late November or early December (reported 13 Nov).
Dammam (DMM)
Operating. Some foreign airlines are restarting Dammam flights, for example Qatar Airways from 14 Nov; several others have cancelled flights into late November (reported 13 to 14 Nov).
Other airports
Madinah, Qassim and Ha'il listed as operating by several foreign airlines; some foreign airline flights to Abha and Taif remain paused (reported 13 to 14 Nov). Check your airline or the airport's website.
Foreign airlines
Many have cancelled Riyadh and Dammam flights into late November, and some into December or January 2027. Dates keep changing. Check your airline's travel notices (reported 13 Nov).

Steps

  1. Check your terminal on the boarding pass or in the airline app on the day you fly. Terminals in Riyadh and Jeddah changed in 2026 and can change again.
  2. Riyadh: the Riyadh Metro Yellow Line serves the airport. The airport lists metro hours as 6 am to midnight. Check the airport website for the stop nearest your terminal.
  3. Jeddah: the Haramain High-Speed Railway station at the airport connects to Makkah and Madinah.
  4. Use the taxis and ride-hailing apps that the airport lists on its website, from the marked pick-up areas.
  5. At drop-off lanes, unload and leave. Waiting longer than the free time brings a fee.
  6. For long trips, compare long-stay parking with a taxi for the round trip.
  7. Pack checked bags in a proper suitcase or box. Jeddah airport said in 2023 that it does not accept round, cloth-wrapped or rope-tied bags, or bags with long straps.

Important: Flight schedules can change at short notice. Check your flight status in the airline app before you leave for the airport.
