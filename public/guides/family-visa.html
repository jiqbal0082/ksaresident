Family

Bringing your family to live with you

Permanent family visa for your wife and children.

General information, not advice. This page summarises publicly available rules in plain language. It is not legal advice or a recommendation to take any action. Procedures, fees and fines change without notice and can differ by case. The official portal below is the only authoritative source.

You need: Iqama with at least 3 months left, an eligible profession, salary registered with GOSI (commonly SAR 3,500 or more), attested marriage and birth certificates.

Key figures

Who you can sponsor
Wife, sons under 18, unmarried daughters
Dependent fee
SAR 400 per dependent per month, paid at iqama renewal

Steps

  1. Check eligibility in Absher. Profession on the iqama decides whether the request is accepted.
  2. Apply for the family visa in Absher, print the form and have your employer attest it through the Chamber of Commerce.
  3. Pay the visa fee (reported as SAR 2,000 per family) and send the visa number to your family.
  4. Your family gets the visa stamped through the visa centre in their country and travels.
  5. Within 90 days of arrival: medical tests, health insurance and their iqama through Absher.

Last checked: 2026-10-24. Confirm current rules on the official source: absher.sa
