Privacy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

What we collect

Nothing you type into the calculators or the scam check leaves your device. These tools run entirely in your browser. We do not ask for your name, iqama number, phone number or email.

What your browser stores

Your language choice is saved in your own browser so the site opens in the same language next time. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Visitor statistics

We may count visits using privacy-friendly analytics that do not use cookies and do not identify you.

Advertising and links

Some pages may show ads or links to partner services. Sponsored content is always labelled. When you follow a link to another site, that site's own privacy policy applies.

Information only

All content is general information, not advice or a recommendation. See the Disclaimer and Terms of use.

Not a government website

KSA Resident is an independent, community-run project. It is not connected to any Saudi government body. Always confirm information on the official portal before you act.
