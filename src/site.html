Labor Law · Articles 84–88

End-of-service calculator

Enter your last monthly wage, your dates and how the job ended.

How the award is worked out

Art. 84

Half a month's wage for each of the first five years, then one month's wage for each year after. Part-years count in proportion. The last wage is used.

Art. 85

If you resign: under 2 years nothing, 2 to 5 years one third, over 5 and under 10 years two thirds, 10 years or more the full award.

Art. 81 · 87

Full award even when you leave yourself: employer breach, force majeure, or a female worker within 6 months of marriage or 3 months of childbirth.

Art. 80 · 54

No award after dismissal for listed misconduct, or when the contract ends during probation.

Estimate only. The result is a general calculation from Labor Law Articles 84–88. Your actual entitlement depends on your contract, wage components and the facts of your case, and is decided by your employer, the Ministry (HRSD) or the labor courts. Use the official HRSD calculator and seek qualified advice before relying on it.
